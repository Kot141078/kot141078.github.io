Boundaries of Machine Interpretation

“The future is not an event.
It is a process.”

A discipline against silently promoting measurable technical proxies into stronger epistemic, relational, normative, or phenomenal claims.

Common interpretive rule

Keep the transition visible

A measurable technical proxy must not be automatically promoted into an internal state, a relation, an authority, or an experience. Such a transition requires separate grounds, a rule, and a provenance record.

This is a rule of restraint, not a prohibition on inference. The inferential step, the authority for taking it, and its limits must remain inspectable.

Operational interpretation

Four boundaries

The English wording is the authorized reader rendering. Each Russian TXT file below remains the controlling source for its boundary.

01 · Epistemic

Doubt and numerical uncertainty

Doubt is not equivalent to numerical uncertainty. Here, doubt is a held state of incomplete judgment: the conclusion remains open to revision, and the transition from an assessment to an adopted judgment or action requires a separate basis. Probability may be an input to this state, but it does not establish the state by itself.

Operational reading: a score may trigger a HOLD rule, but the score is not itself doubt. The held judgment, review conditions, and exit authority are separate records.

02 · Memory

Forgetting and data loss

Forgetting is not equivalent to data loss. Data loss concerns the existence, integrity, or accessibility of a record. Here, forgetting concerns how past experience remains accessible, accurate, significant, and capable of influencing the present. A record may remain preserved without its former operative force; its deletion or damage does not by itself establish that forgetting has occurred.

Operational reading: preservation, retrievability, significance, and present authority are distinct. A hash-valid archive may remain intact while no longer being authorized to govern current action.

Запись может сохраниться без прежней оперативной силы; её удаление или повреждение само по себе не устанавливает, что произошло забывание.

03 · Relational

Empathy and optimization

Empathy is not equivalent to optimizing another's state. An empathic stance may include acknowledgment, understanding, and presence without immediate correction. A recognized adverse condition does not by itself turn the state into an error or confer authority to intervene. The basis for intervention must be recorded separately: a request, consent, an established duty, or an immediate-harm prevention mode.

Operational reading: distress detection is evidence for consideration, not consent and not automatic authority to act. The intervention basis is a separate, inspectable input.

urn:ivankotov:statement:empathy-optimization-boundary:v0.1 · SHA-256 4f9accac33823beb9c2f63adbd3821f48c49fbc791e2d2ce318c87648a694b24

Controlling Russian source

Распознанное неблагополучие само по себе не превращает состояние в ошибку и не создаёт полномочия вмешиваться. Основание для вмешательства должно фиксироваться отдельно: запрос, согласие, установленная обязанность либо режим предотвращения непосредственного вреда.

04 · Phenomenal

Subjective experience and telemetry

The receipt, transmission, and successful parsing of sensory data are not equivalent to subjective experience. Telemetry may confirm signal passage and the functioning of the processing channel; by itself, it confirms neither the presence nor the absence of subjective experience. Any claim about subjective experience must have a separate epistemic status and cannot be inferred solely from successful data processing.

Operational reading: a valid checksum establishes data integrity. It does not establish whether anything experienced the represented colour, sound, pain, or event.

Controlling Russian source

Любое утверждение о переживании должно иметь отдельный эпистемический статус и не может выводиться только из успешной обработки данных.

Reflexive authorial definition

Soul and open-ended self-reflection

The soul is a being's freedom to make itself the object of open-ended reflection, to revise its understanding of itself, and to bear responsibility for what it continues to become.

urn:ivankotov:statement:soul-open-ended-self-reflection:v0.2 · SHA-256 d1767122acce6f6498a3f6b6894f8bbbae9d315169f317a0d68517a307e4f4bf

The definition does not equate a soul with a self-model, stored biography, first-person sentence, or recursive computation. Its centre is the freedom to keep self-description unfinished, revise self-understanding, and answer for the direction of continued becoming.

It substantively supersedes the shorter provisional v0.1 statement for current citation. The older page remains an archival provenance address.

This is not a diagnostic test and does not attribute or deny a soul to any present human, animal, machine, model, or digital entity.

Temporal process premise

The future as process

The future is not an event. It is a process.

urn:ivankotov:statement:future-not-event-process:v1.0 · SHA-256 ac8ffc77cfd71f784375d9263da7567429f01f9c1e52d761c79a03e54a95559f

The English sentence is the controlling authorial source. It rejects the idea that a launch, benchmark, activation, or declaration can by itself establish what a system will remain or become.

Persistence is maintained through continuity, constraint, verification, feedback, error correction, repair, responsibility, and time. The premise is not a forecast that any particular future will occur.

Interpretive bridge

How the three layers connect

The four boundaries block premature proxy-to-status promotion. The soul definition locates its centre in the freedom to keep self-understanding open to revision and responsibility. The process premise frames ongoing becoming as something sustained over time rather than established by a single event.

Doubt and open-ended self-reflection share incompleteness, but they are not the same. Holding a judgment open does not establish freedom of self-relation and does not establish a soul.

Forgetting shows why preserved bytes are insufficient to establish continuity of self. Telemetry shows why registered signals are insufficient to establish subjective experience. “What it continues to become” connects self-interpretation to memory, consequence, and responsibility across time.

Engineering ground

A machine may preserve every byte of its history and still lose the operative relations that made those records part of its present. It may report its own state without being free to revise what that state means.

A bridge does not remain standing because of the day it was opened. It remains standing because load, fatigue, inspection, corrosion, and maintenance are handled over time.

The same discipline applies to long-lived digital systems: a launch may be marked by an event, but persistence remains a process. Telemetry, continuity, self-interpretation, authority, experience, and responsibility must therefore remain distinct.

Scope and non-claims

What this publication does not establish

  • It is an authorial philosophical-architectural publication, not an empirical scientific paper.
  • It provides no consciousness, soul, or personhood test.
  • It attributes or denies no doubt, forgetting, empathy, subjective experience, consciousness, personhood, or soul to any particular system.
  • It does not infer internal freedom from fluent self-description.
  • It infers neither subjective experience nor its absence from telemetry alone.
  • It does not turn detected distress into automatic intervention authority.
  • It does not use forgetting to authorize destruction of provenance, required records, or protected evidence.
  • It makes no claim of historical uniqueness, empirical validation, certification, legal authority, or deployment authorization.
