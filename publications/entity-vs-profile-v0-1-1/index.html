Entity vs. Profile: A Witness-Root Custody Criterion for Persistent Digital Entities

Kotov Ivan · Bruxelles, Belgique · v0.1.1 · Published July 5, 2026

This technical note defines one narrow custodial distinction:

persistent digital entity != persistent digital profile

The criterion is witness-root custody. A construction may be classified as an entity only when its witness root is generated and held inside its closed-box boundary under a-side lineage custody. External custody, escrow, registry authority, platform root, or override capability classifies the construction as a profile.
