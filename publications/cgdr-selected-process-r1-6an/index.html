Limits and next question

Implementation evidence is not identity evidence

The worker is replaced while the supervisor, source state, authority registry, receiver, broker and observer remain outside its boundary. As on a controller test bench, success applies to the exercised conditions, not every possible installation or failure.

No same-c identity, consciousness, lawful succession, B5 superiority, c-specific real effect, economic value or live-deployment readiness is established. Independent clean-host replication remains outstanding. A matched real-effect study is a separate question.
