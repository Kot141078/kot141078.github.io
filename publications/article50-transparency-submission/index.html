Article 50 Transparency Submission Pack v0.1

Evidence-chain mapping for AI Act transparency implementation.

Overview

Transparency implementation bridge

Article 50 Transparency Submission Pack v0.1 is a technical evidence-chain contribution related to the European Commission / AI Office consultation on draft Article 50 transparency guidelines under the EU AI Act.

It is intended as a practical implementation bridge for Article 50 transparency obligations: AI interaction disclosure, machine-readable marking, provenance, audit/witness evidence, human review, and responsible actors.

The author submitted an official response through the European Commission EUSurvey questionnaire on 2026-05-20. The administrative confirmation record is retained privately and is not included in the public package.
