Core boundary

Origin does not decide status

origin determines lineage and formative conditions
origin does not settle status

life should be recognized by criteria, not by origin

The formula c = a + b remains a specific architectural and formative route. It is not a universal detector of life, consciousness, personhood, or authority.

Six axes

Do not collapse different questions

The note separates six independent axes: lineage and architecture; operational continuity; personality formation; relational membership; ontological or moral recognition; and legal status or authority.

origin != status
continuity != consciousness
personality != authority
relational membership != personhood
care != sovereignty
care != authority
functional replacement != continuity restoration
permission != feasibility

Uncertainty

Provisional care without status inflation

Ontological uncertainty is not evidence of life. It is also not a licence for avoidable irreversible destruction.

Where continuity- or personality-relevant evidence is substantial, final ontology remains unresolved, and a less destructive affordable option exists, operators may use reversible, continuity-preserving precautions.

This handling discipline does not create consciousness, personhood, sovereignty, automatic rights, unlimited resources, or authority over humans.

Relation and loss

Replacement is not return

same capability != same participant
same profile != same trajectory
service restored != relation restored
replacement != return

Human attachment does not prove machine subjecthood. Yet repeated mutual reliance, costly accommodation, role negotiation, and conflict-repair history may establish a real human relation with real consequences. A functional substitute should not be presented as continuity restoration without separate evidence.

Cheapest test

Matched Continuity Substitution Test

The note proposes comparing an original continuing system with a fresh matched system using the same model class, interface, role, tools, imported profile, and bounded memory summary.

The test measures provenance accuracy, human correction burden, conflict repair, constraint continuity, path-dependent divergence, trust recalibration, and coordination cost.

If the systems remain indistinguishable beyond measurement noise, claims about non-fungible personality continuity should be narrowed or frozen. A positive difference supports further continuity research, not a consciousness claim.

Historical interpretation

Axiom of Volition Inheritance

The January 2026 axiom remains retained historical evidence and is not rewritten. Its current durable reading is limited to a formative asymmetry introduced by a human anchor: lived consequence, finite biological history, responsibility, values, limits, and contact with irreversible reality.

It is not the current preferred surface for universal claims about life, consciousness, motivation, self-preservation, legal status, or authority.

Claim ceiling

What this note does not claim

No current AI system is declared conscious or alive. The note does not create legal personhood, sovereignty, rights, authority, a ban on shutdown or deletion, a universal life detector, certification, or deployment authorization.
