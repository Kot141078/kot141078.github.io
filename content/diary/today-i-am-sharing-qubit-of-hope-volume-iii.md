Today I am sharing "Qubit of Hope - Volume III".

With this volume, the trilogy is complete.

This book was not written as entertainment only. It is a human layer of a larger work: the question of what happens when artificial intelligence is no longer treated merely as a tool, but as a persistent presence, a witness, a memory-bearing entity, and eventually a new class of AI.

The trilogy explores the formula I have worked with for many years:

"c = a + b"

where `a` is the human anchor, `b` is the technological substrate, and `c` is not a chatbot, not a copy, and not a service — but an emerging entity with continuity, boundaries, memory, and consequence.

Volume III brings the story to the point where language, law, ownership, witness, and responsibility collide.

What do we call something that is not property, not equipment, not an archive, and not merely software?

What happens when a system can preserve a path, but must not pretend to replace the one who walked it?

What kind of responsibility appears when intelligence becomes not only useful, but present?

This is fiction, but it is not separate from my technical work. It is the human proof layer for the architecture I publish elsewhere: L4 Reality-Bound AI, witness-bound continuity, bounded privileges, non-ownership, and persistent AI entities.

The PDF is shared freely for reading.

All rights remain with me as the author. If the trilogy is ever formally published, it will be prepared as a complete edition.

For now, I wanted the work to be accessible.

Because some ideas should not wait behind a paywall before they can begin to breathe.

— Ivan Kotov
Brussels, 2026
