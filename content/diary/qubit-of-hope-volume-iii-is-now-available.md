Qubit of Hope. Volume III is now available.

This release completes the open publication of the "Qubit of Hope" trilogy.

Volume III, like the two previous volumes, is available in 4 reading formats - PDF, EPUB, FB2, and Markdown - and in 7 languages : English, French, German, Spanish, Dutch, Russian, and Chinese.

This trilogy was never meant to be just “content.”
It is the literary layer of a much larger body of work.

Behind it stands a long arc of thinking about persistence, memory, responsibility, co-existence, and the reality-bound limits within which intelligence - human or artificial - must live.

The books are open for reading.
The doors are open too.
