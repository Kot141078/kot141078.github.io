The Next AI Advantage May Be Your Own Experience

A photo taken before a wall is closed can prevent an expensive mistake months later. An old voice note can explain why a decision was made. A record of a failed attempt can save us from repeating it.

Much of what we learn ends up scattered across photographs, messages, notebooks and our own imperfect memory. AI can help us retrieve those records, connect them and put them to work again.

That could make lived experience more valuable. Someone who has managed people, repaired things, written software or dealt with a dissatisfied customer can bring a sense of what matters, where explanations fail and which consequences tend to arrive late. Access to a capable model gives that experience another way to be used.

But useful work still needs a clear task, relevant context, suitable tools and a way to recognise completion. Before buying more intelligence, we should examine how much of the intelligence already available is being wasted by poor organisation.

Then comes the part that survives the conversation.

Once acted on, today's recommendation changes tomorrow's conditions. A lower price affects demand and stock. A promised deadline affects people and the jobs they can accept next. We should evaluate AI across those consequences, including whether corrections carry into later decisions and still help in a new situation.

Our evaluations need the same care. A rising score can reflect a different reviewer or an easier task. We need comparable checks and evidence from actual use: fewer errors, less rework, better decisions.

Otherwise, AI can automate a remarkable amount of activity around an unfinished problem. Plans generate reviews. Reviews generate new plans. Everything looks increasingly professional while the original task waits.

The practical question is simple: after another round of work, what can we now do, understand or deliver that we could not before?

For me, this is an encouraging direction. A person's years of observation, work and mistakes can become usable material for new possibilities. AI can help us find it, question it and act on it.
