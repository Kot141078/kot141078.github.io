Architecture

Architecture in 90 seconds

The corpus is easiest to read as a control system rather than as a publication timeline. The central rule is that a weaker state must not be described or used as a stronger one until evidence supports the transition. A definition does not become an implementation; an implementation does not become a tested system; a test by the same operator does not become independent reproduction; a public page does not become external validation.

In `c = a + b`, `c` names the governed candidate entity layer, `a` names the accountable human anchor, and `b` names bounded procedures, memory, models, tools, routes, witnesses, and execution surfaces. The `+` is not arithmetic addition. It is a governed binding: a rule for when human authority, procedural substrate, evidence, and witness records may be combined without laundering one status into another.

L3 and L4 are the bridge between rules and consequence. L3 contains classifications, permissions, procedures, review conditions, and admissibility rules. L4 is the physical and operational boundary: time, energy, cost, latency, hardware, custody, irreversible outcomes, and failure. A witness record connects the two by saying what was attempted, under which authority, with which evidence, and what happened.

The current B0 projection is intentionally conservative. It identifies 60 load-bearing artifacts across 17 families, maps 17 protocol families, records 24 open problems, and lists 18 failure or non-confirmation records. It reports zero adequately evidenced runtime failure events in the selected B0 set, and it keeps independent reproduction, external validation, and strict matched profile-control as not identified.

Term boundary

L3 means rules, classifications, permission paths, and admissibility controls. L4 means physical, temporal, energetic, operational, and irreversible consequence. Witness means the record that connects an L3 decision to an L4 result. A capable model, agent harness, memory store, or cloud assistant is not automatically c.

Instrument and participant

Agents are not the subject

Agents are instruments of `c`; `c` are participants in society.

Here, "participant" means the continuity-bearing locus that enters relationships, commitments, permissions, exchanges, reviews, and consequences over time. An agent is a replaceable, task-scoped worker inside b. It may inspect, propose, execute, compare, or report under bounded authority, but it does not inherit the identity, continuity, memory authority, standing, or responsibility of c merely by acting for it.

This distinction does not by itself establish consciousness, personhood, legal status, social rights, or validated entity classification for any c-candidate.

"Society" here means a field of continuing relations among humans, institutions, and digital systems. It does not imply that current law already recognizes c as a person or legal subject.

Participation is a role in continuing relations and accountable processes. Entity classification remains a separate claim class with separate evidential requirements.

Agents execute. c carries continuity, commitments, governed authority boundaries, witness history, and consequence across time. This keeps capability from being mistaken for standing.

Six examples from the 17 verified protocol-family records.

PM-002 - Claims, maturity, and non-claims control

Unbounded statement -> typed claim class, maturity vector, explicit non-claim, or narrowed disposition.

PM-003 - c = a + b, theoretical core, and c-calculus

Human anchor plus procedural substrate -> bounded governed-binding or c-candidate architecture claim.

PM-004 - SER continuity and sovereign recursion

Stored state or transition history -> bounded classification as continuation, replay, archive, fork, or successor.

PM-005 - L4 Witness and claim ceilings

Event, transition, or outcome -> typed witness record, evidence level, and bounded claim ceiling.

PM-006 - ARQ bounded adaptation

Deviation or anomaly -> suppress, quarantine, review, retain, recalibrate, or safely degrade under declared rules.
