OP-001 / Critical / PENDING EVIDENCE

Matched profile-control design

Drafting effect
Blocks stronger claim
Problem statement
Can a running c-candidate be compared with a persistent profile under the same substrate, corpus, disturbance stream, interface, and comparable memory, with anchor-custody history isolated?
Why load-bearing
Without this control, formation and continuity observations remain reproducible by a strong profile.
Current handling
Proposed test layer and first-pass non-confirmation.
Required next evidence or decision
Paired implementation, preregistered observables, custody separation, blinded review, independent anchor replication.
Claim ceiling
Differential residual, entity-over-profile, and related stronger claims remain prohibited.

A054 (0661c182bef1), A055 (10ca5bc43c91)
