PM-001 / F01

Corpus control and canonical routing

Controlled transition
Unrouted or duplicated source -> claim-scoped canonical reading route and ownership boundary.
Laundering risk blocked
Path count, repository location, or ownership -> truth, implementation, or validation.
Evidence required
Canonical path or frozen ref; SHA256; declared role; precedence; supersession state; scope-specific owner.
Status vector
S=stable_specification; I=none_identified; T=unknown; R=unknown; V=none_identified; E=no_empirical_claim
Current limitation
Routing and ownership are defined; they do not certify downstream artefacts.
Claim ceiling
A reader can identify the intended entry path and claim owner, but no technical status is elevated.
