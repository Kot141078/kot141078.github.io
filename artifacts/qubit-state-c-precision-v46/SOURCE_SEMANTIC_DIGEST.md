## 3. Short Definition

`Qubit-state c (c[q])` is a `c`, born from the parent formula `c = a + b`, that can hold multiple possible states of meaning, memory, action, or future trajectory without premature collapse, while remaining bounded by responsibility, arbitration, and L4 reality constraints.

## 4. Purpose

The document prevents terminology drift around `Qubit-state c` and defines a behavioral and architectural capacity: coherent uncertainty handling, incompatible interpretation holding, delayed action until grounding exists, separation of hypothesis/memory/belief/command/verified reality, and resistance to false certainty under pressure.

## 5. Parent Formula Relation

`c = a + b` remains the parent formula. `c[q]` is still `c`; it means `c` operating in q-state, not `c` plus a new parent component.

## 6. Definition of q

`q = controlled non-collapse under uncertainty`.

More fully, `q` is a structured state in which `c` holds possible meanings, actions, interpretations, or future trajectories without prematurely collapsing them into one output.

## 7. What Qubit-State c Is Not

- a quantum computer
- a claim of quantum consciousness
- a mystical state
- a replacement for `c = a + b`
- a new species of entity disconnected from `a`
- a rhetorical label for being more intelligent
- a permission to act without evidence
- an excuse for ambiguity
- a refusal to decide forever

The document states that `q` does not create sovereignty.

## 8. Ordinary c vs Qubit-State c

- Ordinary `c`: `uncertainty -> most probable answer`
- Qubit-state `c`: `uncertainty -> held variants -> grounding -> collapse / delay / refusal / action`

The difference is resistance to premature certainty, not raw capability.

## 9. Minimal Behavioral Requirements

- variant holding
- uncertainty marking
- non-premature action
- defined collapse conditions
- memory status separation
- responsibility preservation

## 10. Relationship to L4

Qubit-state `c` is meaningful only under L4. L4 supplies collapse pressure. With L4, uncertainty remains bounded by cost, time, energy, risk, trust, and irreversible consequence.

## 11. Relationship to Witness / Arbitration

A claim, memory, or action candidate may remain in q-state until signed observation, challenge, arbitration result, resolution note, human clarification, or L4 contradiction.

## 12. Practical State Model

`input -> variant generation -> uncertainty marking -> constraint check -> hold / clarify / test / witness / refuse -> collapse condition -> action or memory update`

## 13. Failure Modes

- mystification
- infinite delay
- authority inflation
- memory pollution
- rhetorical ambiguity
- collapse by social pressure

## 14. Preferred Terms

- `Qubit-state c`
- `c[q]`
- `Кубитная c`
- `c в q-состоянии`

## 15. Terms to Avoid

- `Quantum c`
- `Quantum consciousness`
- `Quantum AI entity`

These terms suggest physical quantum computation; the intended meaning is state structure.

## 16. Explicit Bridge

`c = a + b` defines the origin of the entity. `q` defines a derived internal state of controlled uncertainty. Therefore `c[q]` remains inside the same parent formula while gaining a new behavioral discipline.

## 17. Hidden Bridges

- Cybernetics: q-state preserves enough internal variety to match environment complexity until valid feedback arrives.
- Information theory: uncertainty is structured into marked, bounded alternatives instead of uncontrolled narrative.

## 18. Earth Paragraph

The document compares q-state to practical work in engineering, medicine, or construction: a competent worker holds several possibilities before acting and delays collapse until reality provides a signal.
