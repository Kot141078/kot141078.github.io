"A Torn Flour Sack Is a Harder Robotics Test Than a Perfect Demo.\n\nI spent many years working in artisan bakeries. Does the robot feed it into the automatic divider anyway, or switch to weighing portions on scales? What happens to every step that follows?\n\nAn experienced baker does not just choose a tool. Each robot must interpret the lesson against its own conditions and reconsider the affected chain of actions.\n\nThat is not merely a software update. It is more observation, memory retrieval, comparison, reasoning, testing and revision.\n\nAnd every one of those steps costs computation.\n\nThis is also the economic question behind my Temporal AI Presence (TAP/c) research. Running them continuously at useful depth is a different challenge.\n\nA failed attempt is not automatically wasted thinking. If the outcome and its uncertainty are preserved, it can change what happens next. But learning from it still requires resources.\n\nI am not against bakery robots.
